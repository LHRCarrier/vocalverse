Design system · Phone-frame showcase

Showcase

Every screen of VocalVerse, presented inside a device frame. The frame geometry is fixed — 433×882 body, 389.5×843.5 screen area, 55.75 corner radius — and the screens inside are live pages rendered at 390×844, not flattened mock-ups.

Output 2998×2178 (2x) 3 sets · 9 devices Tokens design-tokens.md
1

App · three core screens

Today's home (floating tab bar · streak badge · stat card · full-width segmented control · dotted-timeline task cards), scene conversation (chat bubbles · language-point chips · record button), and the singing report (deep-purple score card · four-dimension stat row · line-by-line scoring).

Forms & controls

Pill inputs across four states (default · filled · focused · disabled), the full button matrix (hover · pressed · disabled · loading · focus, plus secondary on paper and on card, ghost on dark, and the danger variant), and the segmented control with icon buttons — 56px tall, 16px label, 24px icon, tooltip on every icon-only button.

assets/feature-forms-controls.png assets/feature-screens/s1-inputs.html assets/feature-screens/s2-buttons.html assets/feature-screens/s3-controls.html
3

Overlays & states

Image cards with hover-revealed actions, the near-black lightbox that dims everything behind it, and a three-layer composition — empty state underneath, light scrim in the middle, white r-24 dialog on top, with the toast floating above all of it. Click any sheet to open the same pattern full-size.
