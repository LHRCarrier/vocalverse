Feedback

The report is written while you talk.

Coverage target phrases hit vs missed
Rescue two weak turns in a row → the character rephrases instead of stopping

Retry and demo turns do not count toward coverage, so the bar rewards talking rather than repeating.

How It Works

Nothing about a bad turn ends the session.

A turn can be low quality, rescued, or not scored at all; the session still closes at eight turns and still produces a report.
