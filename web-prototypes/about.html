Hello! Welcome to VocalVerse.

VocalVerse is an AI English app built as connected lines rather than a pile of features: practice (scene roleplay and free talk), immersion (an English community and public-domain novel reading), and singing — all of them feeding one learner record.

Practice starts where most learners stall: speaking. Instead of reading aloud alone, you take on a preset scene with an LLM playing the other person, speak for up to 15 seconds a turn, and get per-turn scoring on pronunciation, fluency, and grammar — plus a language-point coverage bar for the phrases the scene was aiming at.

Nothing is thrown away. Every attempt becomes stored data that drives the learner profile, personalized recommendations, and the reports you review — while the community, the bookshelf, and the song library turn practice into something you keep coming back to.
