Product metrics

The four metrics the team reviews.

Demo values. Each one is defined from the events below — if an event is missing, the metric cannot be computed at all.

68%

Click-through

How often a suggested scene, song or chapter is actually opened.

54%

Completion

Sessions that reach five turns or two minutes, whichever comes first.

41%

Interaction

User-initiated turns over the turns the scene asked for; rescue turns count for neither.

12%

Bounce

Sessions that end before the first scored turn.

Instrumentation

Fifteen events, grouped.

Practice page_view · scene_start · recording_start · recording_complete
Scoring score_event · corpus_hit · practice_complete
Support actions fun_action (replay / demo / hint / retry)
Community feed_view · post_open · interact · share

The full set is emitted by the client and the API, and the four metrics above are derived from it — nothing is inferred after the fact.
